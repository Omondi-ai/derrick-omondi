About Me

I am an educator, curriculum designer and development practitioner based in Nairobi, Kenya. Over the past five years I have worked across classrooms, boardrooms and community spaces — designing mentorship programmes, facilitating leadership retreats, building institutional partnerships and delivering instruction across national and international curricula.

I hold a Bachelor of Education (Arts) from the University of Nairobi and a Certificate in Teaching International Curricula from Riara University. I am currently conducting applied research on artificial intelligence in education and civic gamification tools, with a particular focus on communities facing shortages of electricity, internet connectivity and qualified teachers.

Outside of work, you will find me on a long-distance cycling route, organising community walks, or deep in a book. I believe in lifelong learning — not as a phrase, but as a daily practice.

Research & Projects

Applied research at the intersection of education, technology and civic life. Both projects are ongoing and open to collaboration, funding partnerships and academic exchange.

In Progress

Deep Learning for Education in Resource-Constrained Areas

Expected completion: December 2027

SDG 4 — Quality Education SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

This research explores how artificial intelligence and offline-capable technology platforms can deliver quality education in communities facing chronic shortages of electricity, internet connectivity and qualified teachers. The work proposes scalable, low-cost interventions that can be deployed without reliable infrastructure — addressing one of the most persistent barriers to learning equity in sub-Saharan Africa and beyond.

Who this matters to: Governments and education ministries seeking cost-effective solutions for hard-to-reach schools. International NGOs working in fragile or rural contexts. Development banks funding education infrastructure. UN agencies and programmes aligned with SDG 4 and SDG 10.

Collaboration, funding enquiries and academic partnerships are welcome.

In Progress

Gamification and Deep Learning in Civic Education

Expected completion: May 2027

SDG 16 — Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions SDG 4 — Quality Education

This project develops simulation tools that allow citizens — students, voters, community members — to explore the probable consequences of political and ethical decisions in real-world governance contexts. By combining game-based learning with predictive modelling, the research aims to strengthen democratic participation and informed civic responsibility.

Applications: Secondary school civic education curricula. Civil society and community awareness programmes. National electoral commission training tools. University courses in political science and governance.

Open to academic collaboration, pilot school partnerships and civil society interest.

This is not a political project — it is an educational one. The goal is informed citizens, not directed outcomes.

Beyond the Desk

The things that keep me sharp, grounded and curious.

Long-Distance Cycling

Cycling long distances has taught me more about discipline and goal-setting than any course I have taken. When the road gets steep, you do not stop — you shift gear. I apply the same logic to work: break the distance into stages, keep a steady cadence, and finish what you started. I ride routes around Nairobi and beyond, and I am always open to fellow cyclists who want to plan a ride together.

Community Events & Walks

I believe community is built through shared movement — literal and metaphorical. As the coordinator of the monthly Life360 Community Walks, I have seen how a simple walk can open conversations, build trust and shift the energy of a group entirely. I organise and participate in community events regularly, because I think showing up is one of the most underrated leadership skills there is.

Reading & Continuous Learning

I read widely — education theory, development economics, technology ethics, philosophy and the occasional novel that has no practical application whatsoever. Currently exploring the intersection of AI ethics and education policy, and what Rwanda's education transformation holds as a lesson for Kenya. Lifelong learning is not a tagline for me. It is a daily practice.

Causes I Care About

Education equity. Environmental sustainability. Democratic participation. These are not just causes I advocate — they shape the research I pursue, the programmes I design and the organisations I choose to work with. I am drawn to roles and partnerships that take these issues seriously, whether at the local community level or through international frameworks like the United Nations SDGs.
